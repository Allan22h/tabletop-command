Tabletop Command music options

Saved for later. In the game now: A3 Night march as the menu music.

Everything here is written for the game and synthesized live, with no recordings, so nothing needs a license. The bugle only plays its natural notes (C, G, C, E, G, high C), which already sit on the five-note scale typical of Chinese music. The drums are a deep war drum and a snare, and a gong closes it.

Menu drum loops: tap to start, tap again to stop

A · War-drum march

96 bpm. Steady war drum with snare and rim clicks; every fourth bar builds with a roll, sometimes into a soft gong.

A2 · Tense march

92 bpm. March A, sparer, over a low two-note drone that grinds a half step apart. A radar ping with echo every other bar and a slow radar sweep before each fill.

A3 · Night march

84 bpm. Muffled drums with a ticking rim, a deep drone that pulses slowly, and a radar sweep and double ping once each round, like a scope at night.

A3b · Deep watch

76 bpm. Slower and emptier than A3. A lower drone with a deep boom at the top of each round; one long sweep and a single far-off ping with a long echo.

A3c · Night patrol

84 bpm. A3 with a steady low-drum step under it, like boots moving, a darker minor drone, and pings that come from the left, then the right.

A3d · Silent running

80 bpm. Barely-there drums, a heartbeat and ticking rim, every other bar almost empty. Lower, sonar-style pings with long tails. The quietest one.

A3e · Storm front

84 bpm. A3 plus distant rumbles like far-off artillery or thunder, an extra sweep every other round, and a big low hit and soft gong at the end of each round.

A4 · Closing in

96 bpm. March A over a running low-drum pulse, a thin high tone that rises in each fill, and radar pings that get closer together through the round.

B · Heavy drums

72 bpm. Slow and huge: big low hits with space between, answered by rim clicks, a deep boom and a gong swell every few bars. Epic, cinematic.

C · Drumline cadence

116 bpm. Snare-led parade cadence with flams and accents over a bass drum on the beat. Brighter and more upbeat.

D · Ops room

88 bpm. Quiet and tense: a low drum pulse like a heartbeat, a ticking rim, brushed snare, and a swell every fourth bar. Sits under menus without getting tiring.

Victory

About 10 seconds. A triplet call, a climbing line, then a long high note over a gong.

Raid call

About 4 seconds. Two short calls over a war-drum beat, for starting a raid.

Defeat

About 5 seconds. The same call played slowly and falling, with a muffled drum.

Parts: two bugles (sawtooth tone, a brassy filter that opens on each note, a small scoop up into the pitch, slight vibrato on long notes) · war drum (low thump that drops in pitch) · snare rolls · gong (several out-of-tune partials that swell and fade over 4 s).
